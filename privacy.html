Privacy policy

charge-line · last updated 15 October 2026

charge-line is a personal, non-commercial project: an iPhone app that plans electric-vehicle road trips and sends them to the driver's own Tesla. It is used only with vehicles its developer owns or is authorised to control, and is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

When you sign in with your Tesla account and approve it, charge-line receives OAuth tokens with the scopes openid, offline_access, vehicle_device_data and vehicle_cmds. It uses them only to:

  • list the vehicles on your account;
  • check whether the vehicle has approved charge-line's key and which command protocol it uses;
  • read the battery level and charge limit, and the live stall counts of nearby Superchargers;
  • wake the vehicle, when you ask; and
  • send a trip you planned (destination and charging stops) to the vehicle's navigation.

Where data is kept

Your Tesla tokens are stored only on your iPhone, in its Keychain. The app's requests to Tesla pass through charge-line's server (hosted on Fly.io in Sydney), which adds the app's credentials and signs commands with charge-line's key. The server stores no tokens, vehicle data or trips; its logs record only the kind of request, its result and how long it took.

To plan a trip, the app sends the start (your location) and destination to Google Maps Platform (Routes API and Places API) and the planned route line to Open Charge Map, through the server, to find chargers along it. Your profile (car, battery and preferences) stays on your iPhone. The stops you send go to Tesla to set the vehicle's navigation.

charge-line does not sell or share data, and this website uses no cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visits as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Removing access

Sign out of Tesla in the app's Settings to delete the tokens from your iPhone. Remove charge-line's key from the vehicle's Locks screen, and revoke its access in your Tesla account's security settings.
