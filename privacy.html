Privacy policy

Charge Line · last updated 28 October 2026

Charge Line is an iPhone app, with CarPlay, that plans electric-vehicle road trips in Australia and can send them to the driver's own Tesla. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it doesn't sell or share your data.

What stays on your iPhone

Your car and battery profile, preferences, saved trips, Home and Work, recent searches and the trip records used to learn how your car drives are stored only on your iPhone. A recent search is only the text you typed. For Home or Work chosen from Google, only Google's place ID is kept, with its location for at most 30 days. The counts of how much of the free plan you've used this month and today are also kept only on your iPhone, in its Keychain, so that reinstalling the app doesn't reset them.

What is sent to other services

To plan trips and find chargers, the app sends only what each request needs, when you use it:

  • Your location, with your permission, to start a trip from where you are and to find chargers near you on the iPhone or in CarPlay. It is sent with those requests and isn't stored by Charge Line.
  • Google Maps Platform (Routes API, Places API and the Maps SDK) receives your start, destination and stops to plan the route, the route or your location to find chargers and places to eat, and the text you type when searching for a place. Google's handling is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Open Charge Map receives the route line or your location, through Charge Line's server, to find more chargers.
  • Amazon Web Services serves the public elevation data the app downloads for the area of your route, to estimate the energy used on hills.
  • When you tap Navigate or Directions, the stop is handed to Google Maps or Apple Maps on your iPhone.

Tesla

Connecting a Tesla is optional. When you sign in with your Tesla account and approve it, Charge Line receives OAuth tokens with the scopes openid, offline_access, vehicle_device_data and vehicle_cmds. It uses them only to:

  • list the vehicles on your account;
  • check whether the vehicle has approved Charge Line's key and which command protocol it uses;
  • read the battery level and charge limit, and the live stall counts of nearby Superchargers;
  • wake the vehicle, when you ask; and
  • send a trip you planned (destination and charging stops) to the vehicle's navigation.

Your Tesla tokens are stored only on your iPhone, in its Keychain.

Charge Line's server

Requests to Tesla and Open Charge Map pass through Charge Line's server, hosted on Fly.io in Sydney, which adds the app's credentials and signs Tesla commands with Charge Line's key. The server stores no tokens, locations, vehicle data or trips; its logs record only the kind of request, its result and how long it took, plus an error message when a request fails.

Purchases

Charge Line Premium is bought through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles the payment, and Charge Line never sees your payment details or Apple Account. The app receives from Apple only what it needs to unlock Premium: which plan you bought, when it started and ends, and whether it was refunded. Apple's handling is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Removing your data

  • Deleting the app removes the data it stores on your iPhone, except Keychain items. Sign out of Tesla in the app's Settings first to delete your Tesla tokens.
  • Remove Charge Line's key from the vehicle's Locks screen, and revoke its access in your Tesla account's security settings.
  • Cancel a subscription in Settings › Manage subscription or in your Apple Account settings.
