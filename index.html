Charge Line

Charge Line is an iPhone app that plans electric-vehicle road trips in Australia and can send a planned trip — destination and charging stops — to the driver's own Tesla through Tesla's Fleet API.

This site hosts the app's public key, which Tesla requires for registration and for owners to approve the app's virtual key:

/.well-known/appspecific/com.tesla.3p.public-key.pem
